**Subject: Inventory-sync integration for multi-listed venues — request for API access or written consent**

Hi [name],

I'm building TurfSync, a tool that helps sports-venue owners who list on
multiple booking platforms (Playo, Hudle, KheloMore, and their own direct
channel) avoid double-booking the same court across them. It works entirely
from the owner's own side — reading the booking notifications they already
receive, and prompting staff to block a slot on the other platforms when one
sells — so venues stay in sync without needing anything from you today.

I'd like to ask two questions before building anything closer to Playo:

1. **Does Playo have a partner or developer API** — even a limited one shared
   with strategic partners rather than publicly documented — that would let
   an inventory-sync tool read bookings and block/unblock a slot on a venue's
   behalf, with the venue's own authorization?

2. **If not, would Playo grant written consent** for a narrow, fully
   disclosed integration: read-only booking visibility plus block/unblock on
   a single mapped court, rate-limited, with full audit logging on our side,
   operated only for venues that have explicitly opted in? Your own terms
   note this path exists ("without express written consent from TechMash"),
   which is why I'm asking directly rather than assuming either yes or no.

This is framed as an inventory-sync layer, not a competing booking
marketplace for players — the goal is fewer double-bookings and fewer
support escalations on your side too, since a slot double-sold on Playo
because it wasn't blocked elsewhere is a refund and a bad review for both of
us.
